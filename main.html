Paris, France


Paris, the capital city of France, is renowned for its rich history, stunning architecture, world-class art, and delectable cuisine. Often called the "City of Light," Paris offers an enchanting atmosphere that draws millions of visitors every year.

  1. Eiffel Tower – The iconic symbol of Paris with breathtaking city views.
  2. Louvre Museum – The world’s largest art museum and home of the Mona Lisa.
  3. Notre-Dame Cathedral – A masterpiece of Gothic architecture.
  4. Champs-Élysées – A world-famous avenue for shopping and dining.
  5. Montmartre – A charming hilltop district known for the Sacré-Cœur Basilica.
  • Croissant – Flaky, buttery pastry perfect for breakfast.
  • Macarons – Colorful, delicate meringue-based confections.
  • Escargot – A traditional dish of garlic butter snails.
  • Crêpes – Thin pancakes with a variety of sweet or savory fillings.
  • French Onion Soup – A rich soup topped with melted cheese and bread.
